<objective>
Import transferdata final.csv (~47,252 rows) into the Transfer table, resolving club from the Club column (not the trap UniqueID), keeping dealing_club as a plain string, best-effort name-matching the player FK, and upserting idempotently on a 6-column composite event key.

Purpose: DATA-04 (real transfer history, queryable). The single highest-risk trap in this phase: transferdata.UniqueID is a CLUB id (207 distinct, 1:1 with Club), NOT a player id — joining it to Player.UniqueID silently attaches transfers to the wrong players. This plan encodes and TESTS the correct behavior explicitly.
Output: import_transfers.py + transfers/tests/test_transfer_import.py.
</objective>
