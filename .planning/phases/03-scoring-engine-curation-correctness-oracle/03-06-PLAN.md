not just the raw RF (03-RESEARCH.md "Reproduction note").

Cross-plan wiring (why this plan depends on 04 & 05): the 33-feature recipe includes
`player_impact`, `performance_score`, `compatibility_score`, and `role_pct`. In the original
monolithic script these are already COLUMNS on the players dataframe by the time
`build_transfer_value_dataset` merges players into transfers. They are NOT produced by
`reconstruct.build_players_df()` — they come from Plan 04 (`compute_rmm_column`) and Plan 05
(`compute_cs_tp_for_pairs`). So faithful reproduction REQUIRES merging those 4 columns onto
`players_df` first; otherwise the source's `[c for c in feature_cols if c in model_df.columns]`
line silently drops them and the recipe is no longer the real 33-feature recipe. This plan
therefore depends on 04 & 05 (wave 3) and does the merge explicitly in its management command.

artifact + recorded MAE/R², and a test proving the artifact reloads and predicts.
